## 🔑 Key Design Principles

### 1. **Progressive Disclosure**
- Start simple (one sentence)
- Expand on click (medium detail)
- Full research available (detailed methodology)

### 2. **Superscript Numbers**
- Non-intrusive
- Clear what's cited
- Easy to click/tap

### 3. **Student-Friendly Language**
- No academic jargon
- Normal English
- Concrete examples

### 4. **Credibility Indicators**
- ✓ Verified dates
- Source authority (Dean > CDS > Analysis)
- Confidence levels (Very High, High, Medium)

### 5. **Visual Hierarchy**
- Most important citations first
- Dean quotes highlighted
- Official data emphasized

| Claim Type | Example | Citation Provided |
