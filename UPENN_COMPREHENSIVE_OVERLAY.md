## Penn's Essay Philosophy and Unique "Accepting Influence" Framework

### Essays Rated "Very Important" - Unusual Among Ivies

**CRITICAL CONTEXT**: Penn rates Application Essays as **"Very Important"** in CDS Section C7, placing them in the SAME tier as:
- Rigor of secondary school record
- Academic GPA
- Recommendations
- Character/personal qualities

**Dean Jordan Pascucci on Essay Role**:
> "The quality of thought and expression in the application essay... A bad essay will not get you not admitted. And a great essay has never gotten anyone admitted... Essays should be consistent with the rest of the materials you're presenting but not redundant."

**Translation**: Essays cannot overcome weak academics, but weak essays can hurt strong applicants. Essays must add NEW, non-redundant information about thinking quality and character.

### The Revolutionary "Thank You Note" - Penn's Unique Prompt

**Full Prompt**: "Write a short thank-you note to someone you have not yet thanked and would like to acknowledge. (We encourage you to share this note with that person, if possible, and reflect on the experience!)"

**Why This Exists - Dean Whitney Soule's Explanation**:

> "We were looking for a prompt that would give students the opportunity to show us that they could **accept positive influence from other people**. It's just a central component to being able to collaborate and learn... Accepting influence is essential to relationships, to building community, and of course to learning."
