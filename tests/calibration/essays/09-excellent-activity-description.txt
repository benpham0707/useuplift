When our school cafeteria switched to compostable trays, 83% ended up in the regular trash. I spent two weeks standing next to the bins during lunch, counting. Then I built a sorting station with color-coded signage in three languages, trained 14 student volunteers as "waste coaches," and partnered with the district's sustainability coordinator to track diversion rates weekly. Contamination dropped from 83% to 19% in one semester. The district adopted our signage system across all seven middle and high schools, diverting an estimated 4.2 tons of compostable material from landfill annually. I learned that environmental change is not an awareness problem. It is a design problem. People will do the right thing if you make the right thing the easy thing.
