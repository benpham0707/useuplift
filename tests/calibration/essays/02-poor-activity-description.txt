As president of the Environmental Club, I led our team of dedicated members in various activities to help the environment. We organized weekly cleanups at local parks and beaches. I was responsible for coordinating meetings, delegating tasks, and communicating with school administration. Under my leadership, we grew the club from 10 to 25 members. I also organized our annual Earth Day event which was very successful. Being president taught me the importance of leadership, teamwork, and making a difference in my community. This experience has made me passionate about environmental sustainability and I hope to continue this work in college.
