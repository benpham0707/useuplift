## Caltech's Essay Philosophy and Unique "Maker Mindset" Framework

### Essays Rated "Important" - Below Academic Thresholds

**CRITICAL CONTEXT**: Caltech rates Application Essays as **"Important"** in CDS Section C7, placing them BELOW:
- Rigor of secondary school record (Very Important)
- Standardized test scores (Very Important)
- Character/personal qualities (Very Important)

But EQUAL to:
- Academic GPA (Important)
- Extracurricular activities (Important)

**Why This Matters**:


**Ashley Pallie (Director) on Essay Function**:
> "The [essay/interview] is a gauge of authenticity... Can you claim this research?"

**Translation**: Essays test OWNERSHIP of intellectual work. Listing a research project fails - you must explain the process, the failures, and the questions that drove you.

## Caltech Essay Structure Overview

**Total Essays Required**: 3 required essays + 2 short answer selections
1. **STEM Interest** (100-200 words) - Required
2. **STEM Experience/Curiosity** (100-200 words) - Required
3. **Creativity/Innovation** (100-200 words) - Required
4. **Short Answer #1** (up to 125 words) - Select from 4 options
5. **Short Answer #2** (up to 125 words) - Select from 4 options (different from #1)
6. **Common App Personal Statement** (650 words) - All applicants

**Total Word Count**: ~600-800 words across Caltech supplements + 650 Common App

**Short Answer Options** (Select 2):
- Hobby or creative pursuit for fun
- Class you'd teach
- Something that influences how you see world
- Concept or topic that blows your mind
