## Part 3: Core Findings Needing Deeper Understanding

### CF-1: The "Billions of Combinations" Framework (3.7)
**Current Understanding:** Mathematical reality prevents simple ranking; class building explains variable outcomes.
**Needs Deeper Understanding:**
- How committee deliberation actually operationalizes this
- What specific "types" are tracked and balanced
- How waitlist decisions relate to class composition
- Role of yield prediction in class building

### CF-2: The 11-Point Letter Grading System (3.3)
**Current Understanding:** UNC uses A+ to D scale for applications.
**Needs Deeper Understanding:**
- How other institutions grade/score applications
- What distinguishes each grade level specifically
- How grades map to admit/waitlist/deny decisions
- Inter-rater reliability and calibration

### CF-3: The "During" Architecture (3.6)
**Current Understanding:** Maturity shown in process, not outcome; "before-during-after" framework.
**Needs Deeper Understanding:**
- Specific examples of strong "during" passages
- Temporal markers signaling transformation
- Counter-examples of weak attempts
- Length/proportion guidelines

### CF-4: Peer Impact Quantification (3.7)
**Current Understanding:** Peer persistence effect (1.8%) is 2x peer GPA effect (0.9%).
**Needs Deeper Understanding:**
- Which specific traits drive peer influence
- How institutions predict peer impact at admission
- Longitudinal outcomes data informing decisions
- Peer composition optimization methods

### CF-5: The Three-Category Intersection (3.7)
**Current Understanding:** Academic, Personal, Contextual evaluated together, not separately.
**Needs Deeper Understanding:**
- How officers operationalize intersection in real-time
- Weight given to context vs raw achievement
- Training for consistent intersection evaluation
- Edge cases where categories conflict

### CF-6: Recommendation Letter Power (3.3)
**Current Understanding:** 30% rate letters "highly influential."
**Needs Deeper Understanding:**
- What specific phrases are most impactful
- How officers read "between the lines"
- How "code phrases" are interpreted
- What distinguishes good/great/exceptional letters

### CF-7: The "Tip Factor" System (3.5)
**Current Understanding:** "Tips and hooks" provide admission advantage.
**Needs Deeper Understanding:**
- Full taxonomy of recognized tip factors
- How tips are weighted against each other
- Threshold effects (when tip becomes decisive)
- Institutional variation in tip priorities

### CF-8: Essay Evidence Hierarchy (3.3)
**Current Understanding:** "Show don't tell" is paramount.
**Needs Deeper Understanding:**
- Specific examples of strong vs weak evidence
- How officers distinguish genuine detail from fiction
- Optimal balance of scene vs reflection
- Word count allocation best practices
