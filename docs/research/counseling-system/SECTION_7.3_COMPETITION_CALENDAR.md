# Section 7.3: Competition Calendar & Registration Deadlines

**Purpose:** Comprehensive calendar of major competitions, registration deadlines, and key dates for college-bound students.

**System Usage:** Query by month or category to alert students to upcoming deadlines and opportunities.

**Last Updated:** January 2026

**Note:** Dates are approximate and should be verified annually. Most competitions update dates 2-3 months in advance.

---

## Calendar Overview

### Critical Insight: Timing Is Everything

> "The students who succeed at the highest levels aren't just talented—they know when to apply, when to register, and when to prepare."
> — CollegeBase

**Common Timing Mistakes:**
- Missing registration deadlines (often 2-3 months before competition)
- Not allowing enough preparation time
- Discovering opportunities too late
- Trying to prepare for too many simultaneous competitions
