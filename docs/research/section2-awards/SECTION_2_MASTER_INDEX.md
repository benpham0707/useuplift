## Award Tier Quick Reference

### Tier 1: Exceptional (National/International Recognition)
**Selectivity:** <2% acceptance rate, typically <500 recipients nationally
**Points:** 4
**Admissions Impact:** Major - Can significantly differentiate candidate

| Award | Selectivity | Annual Recipients | Impact |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|--------|
| Regeneron STS Finalist | 1.6% | 40 | Major |
| ISEF Grand Award | <0.01% (of 7M) | ~100 | Major |
| USAMO Qualifier | <0.01% | ~260 | Major |
| IMO/IPhO/IChO Medal | <0.001% | 6/country | Major |
| Davidson Fellow | 2-4% | ~20 | Major |
| U.S. Presidential Scholar | <0.01% | 161 | Major |
| YoungArts Winner w/ Distinction | ~1% | ~170 | Major |
| Scholastic National Gold Medal | 0.2% | ~700 | Major |

### Tier 2: Outstanding (State/Regional Excellence)
**Selectivity:** 1-5% acceptance rate, 500-5,000 recipients nationally
**Points:** 3
**Admissions Impact:** Moderate to Major - Strong differentiator

| Award | Selectivity | Annual Recipients | Impact |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|--------|
| Regeneron STS Scholar | 12-14% | 300 | Major |
| ISEF Finalist | <1% (of regional) | 1,800 | Major |
| AIME Qualifier | ~5% (of AMC) | 6,000-7,000 | Moderate |
| National Merit Semifinalist | 1% | 16,000 | Moderate |
| RSI/TASP Attendee | 2-3% | 80-100 | Major |
| Eagle Scout/Gold Award | 4-8% (of scouts) | ~61,000 | Moderate |
| All-State Athletic | 1-2% (of athletes) | Varies | Moderate |
| TOC Qualifier | Selective | ~500 | Moderate |

### Tier 3: Strong (State/Regional Recognition)
**Selectivity:** 5-15% selection rate
**Points:** 2
**Admissions Impact:** Minor to Moderate - Supports narrative

| Award | Selectivity | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| State Science Fair Top 3 | ~5% | Strong for STEM |
| Scholastic Regional Gold Key | ~7% | Strong for arts |
| AP Scholar with Distinction | 4-10% | Common among competitive |
| State academic team | 5-10% | Subject-dependent |
| All-Conference Athletic | Varies | Baseline athletic |

### Tier 4: Baseline (Local/School Recognition)
**Selectivity:** 15-50% or merit threshold
**Points:** 1
**Admissions Impact:** Minor - Expected among competitive applicants

| Award | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| National Honor Society membership | 60-80% of T20 applicants have |
| AP Scholar | 28% of AP students |
| Honor Roll / Dean's List | Common |
| School-level awards | Baseline |
| Participation awards | Minimal value |

## Key Principles from Research

### 1. Selectivity Determines Impact
> "The greater impact the claim has on your potential as an applicant, the more likely schools will do some fact-checking."
> — Sara Harberson

Awards follow the same 4-tier system as activities, with selectivity being the primary determinant of tier classification.

### 2. Context Calibration Matters
> "A student earning All-State recognition from a competitive state (California, Texas, New York) carries more weight than the same recognition from a less competitive state."

Geographic and demographic context modifies award assessment just as it does for activities.

### 3. National Merit is Baseline, Not Differentiator
> "At highly selective institutions accepting fewer than 20% of applicants, NHS membership appears on 60-80% of applications, making it expected rather than exceptional."

Common awards among competitive applicants (National Merit, NHS, AP Scholar) confirm baseline competence but don't differentiate.

### 4. Tier 1 Awards Can "Tip" Decisions
> "Tournament of Champions winners gain significant advantages in selective college admissions. Yale, Harvard, Stanford, and Princeton actively recruit successful TOC competitors."

Tier 1 awards (top 500 nationally) can move applications from "competitive" to "admit" in borderline cases.

### 5. Award-Activity Coherence Strengthens Both
> "Awards that connect to sustained activities create stronger narratives than isolated achievements."

Awards should reinforce the spike/theme demonstrated by activities, not appear disconnected.
