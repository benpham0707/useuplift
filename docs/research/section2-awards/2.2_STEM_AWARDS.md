## Overview

STEM awards carry significant weight in college admissions, particularly for STEM-focused institutions like MIT, Caltech, and Carnegie Mellon. The key differentiator is the level of original work required—research-based awards (Regeneron, ISEF) rank highest, followed by olympiad-style competitions, then team academic competitions.

### Key Principles

1. **Research > Competition:** Original research awards are valued more than competitive exams
2. **Verification is high:** STEM awards are easily verifiable through results databases
3. **School-specific preferences:** MIT/Caltech value these highly; liberal arts less so
4. **Progression matters:** AIME → USAMO → IMO shows clear trajectory
5. **Context calibration:** Access to preparation and resources is considered

> "MIT admissions data indicates 23% of enrolled students participated in science fairs, with ISEF finalists comprising 8% and major award winners 2% of admits."

## Academic Olympiads

### Mathematics Olympiad Pathway

```
AMC 10/12 → AIME → USAMO/USAJMO → MOP → IMO
   ↓           ↓         ↓           ↓      ↓
Tier 4     Tier 2    Tier 1      Tier 1  Tier 1
```

| Level | Award | Selectivity | Annual Recipients | Tier |
|-------|-------|-------------|-------------------|------|
| AMC 10/12 | Top 2.5-5% | Merit threshold | ~100,000 | 4 |
| AIME Qualifier | Top 5% of AMC | 5-7% | 6,000-7,000 | 2 |
| USAMO/USAJMO Qualifier | Top 500 nationally | <0.01% | ~500 | 1 |
| USAMO Winner (Top 12) | Best in nation | Top 12 | 12 | 1 |
| MOP Invitee | Training camp | Top 60 | ~60 | 1 |
| IMO Team Member | International team | Top 6 | 6 | 1 |
| IMO Medal | International competition | Top 50% of teams | ~300 globally | 1 |

**Admissions Impact:**
- **AIME Qualifier:** Moderate - Shows strong math ability, expected for MIT/Caltech applicants
- **USAMO Qualifier:** Major - Top 250 math students nationally, significant differentiator
- **IMO Medal:** Transformative - "Virtually guarantees admission consideration at any school"

> "A USAMO qualifier has demonstrated mathematical ability in the top 0.01% of students. This is one of the strongest credentials a math-interested student can present."

## Science Fairs & Research

### Regeneron Science Talent Search (STS)

The most prestigious pre-college science competition in the United States.

| Level | Selectivity | Recipients | Prize | Tier |
|-------|-------------|------------|-------|------|
| Entrant | Application | ~2,500 | - | 4 |
| Scholar | Top 300 | 300 (~12%) | $2,000 | 2 |
| Finalist | Top 40 | 40 (~1.6%) | $25,000+ | 1 |
| Top 10 | Top 10 | 10 | $40,000-250,000 | 1 |

**Selection Criteria:**
- Original research project (must be individual work)
- Research paper quality
- Letters of recommendation
- Essays and test scores
- Extracurricular activities

> "STS Finalists have gone on to receive 14 Nobel Prizes, 5 National Medals of Science, and numerous MacArthur Fellowships."

**Admissions Impact:**
- **Scholar:** Strong credential, indicates serious research ability
- **Finalist:** "One of the strongest research credentials a HS student can have"
- **Top 10:** Transformative - opens doors to any institution

### Regeneron ISEF (International Science and Engineering Fair)

The world's largest pre-college science competition.

| Level | Scope | Recipients | Tier |
|-------|-------|------------|------|
| Local/Regional Fair | School/County | Varies | 4 |
| State Fair | State level | Varies | 3 |
| ISEF Finalist | International | ~1,800 | 2 |
| Category Award | Category winner | ~300 | 1 |
| Best of Category | Category 1st | 22 | 1 |
| Grand Award | Top overall | ~100 | 1 |

**Selection Pathway:**
```
School Fair → Regional Fair → State Fair → ISEF
    ↓             ↓              ↓           ↓
 Varies      Top 10-20%      Top 5-10%    <1%
```

> "Each year, an estimated 7 million students globally participate in affiliated fairs, with roughly 1,800 finalists (about 0.03%) advancing to ISEF."

**Admissions Impact:**
- **ISEF Finalist:** Strong - competed at highest level
- **Category Award:** Major - top in specific field
- **Grand Award:** Transformative - "equivalent to international olympiad gold medals"

### Research vs Competition Trade-offs

| Factor | Research Awards | Competition Awards |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Demonstrates | Original thinking, persistence | Speed, knowledge depth |
| Time investment | 6+ months | Ongoing training |
| Verification | Paper/poster exists | Results databases |
| Essay material | Rich stories | Limited narratives |
| School preference | Stanford, Harvard favor | MIT, Caltech favor |

### The "Expected" vs "Differentiating" Threshold

For MIT/Caltech applicants:
- **Expected:** AIME qualification, USACO Silver+, Science Olympiad states
- **Differentiating:** USAMO, USACO Platinum, ISEF Finalist, Regeneron Scholar

For Stanford/Harvard applicants:
- **Expected:** Strong academics, some competition success
- **Differentiating:** Research publications, Regeneron STS, original projects

## TypeScript Interfaces

```typescript
interface STEMAwardAssessment {
  award: string;
  category: 'olympiad' | 'research' | 'science_fair' | 'computing' | 'robotics' | 'academic_team' | 'program';

  // Pathway position (for olympiads)
  pathway?: {
    name: string;
    level: string;
    nextLevel?: string;
    ultimateLevel: string;
    positionInPathway: number; // 1-5
  };

  // Research-specific (for research awards)
  researchComponent?: {
    originalityLevel: 'groundbreaking' | 'novel' | 'incremental' | 'replication';
    publicationStatus: 'published' | 'submitted' | 'in_progress' | 'none';
    mentorAffiliation: string;
    fieldRelevance: string;
  };

  // Competition-specific
  competitionMetrics?: {
    participantPool: number;
    placementPercentile: number;
    yearAchieved: number;
    repeatAchievement: boolean;
  };

  // School-specific value
  schoolSpecificValue: Record<string, {
    schoolId: string;
    valueLevel: 'transformative' | 'major' | 'moderate' | 'minor' | 'expected';
    reasoning: string;
  }>;

  tier: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4;
  admissionsImpact: 'transformative' | 'major' | 'moderate' | 'minor';
}

interface OlympiadPathway {
  name: string; // e.g., "USA Math Olympiad Pathway"
  levels: {
    levelName: string;
    selectivity: string;
    annualRecipients: number;
    tier: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4;
    prerequisite?: string;
    nextLevel?: string;
  }[];
  ultimateAchievement: string;
  internationalEquivalent?: string;
}

interface ResearchProgramAssessment {
  programName: string;
  acceptanceRate: number;
  annualParticipants: number;
  duration: string;
  institution: string;

  selectionCriteria: {
    academicWeight: number;
    researchInterestWeight: number;
    essayWeight: number;
    recommendationWeight: number;
  };

  outcomes: {
    collegeAdmissionBoost: string;
    typicalAdmitProfile: string;
    notableAlumni?: string[];
  };

  tier: 1 | 2;
}
```
