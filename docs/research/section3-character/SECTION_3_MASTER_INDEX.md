## Core Philosophy

### Character is Separately Rated, Not an Afterthought

**Critical Finding:** At elite institutions, character dimensions are NOT subjective impressions but **CODIFIED, SEPARATELY RATED DIMENSIONS** with specific evaluation criteria.

Stanford's tripartite system exemplifies this:
- Academic Rating (1-6 scale)
- Extracurricular Rating (1-6 scale)
- **Intellectual Vitality Rating (1-6 scale)** - INDEPENDENT

### The "Ooze" Standard

> "Intellectual vitality must OOZE from the file"
> — Dr. Irena Smith, Former Stanford AO

Character qualities must permeate the entire application, not appear as isolated claims.
