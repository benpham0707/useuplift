## PART 1: PROJECT PHILOSOPHY AND STANDARDS

### Core Philosophy: "Depth First. Quality Above All. Each Step Feeds the Next."

This is NOT surface-level research. We are building a system that will advise students on one of the most important decisions of their lives. Every finding must be:

1. **Grounded in authority** - Named sources, specific institutions, documented policies
2. **Actionable** - Can be translated into system logic and user guidance
3. **Honest about limitations** - Acknowledge what we don't know, rate quality fairly
4. **Cross-referenced** - Connect findings across sections to build coherent understanding

### Quality Standards

**A-Grade Research Requires:**
- 50+ unique, credible citations per major topic
- Direct quotes from admissions officers BY NAME when possible
- Specific examples (real cases, documented outcomes)
- Quantified data where available (percentages, thresholds, statistics)
- School-specific policies with official statements
- Clear categorization (what works vs. what doesn't)

**Red Flags for Poor Research:**
- Generic advice without attribution
- Vague language ("many schools" without naming them)
- No specific examples or case studies
- Missing quantification of thresholds or impacts
- Outdated information (pre-2023 for SFFA-related topics)

### Research Process Standards

For EACH gap being filled:

1. **Deep Sequential Thinking** - Use 5+ thought steps to analyze critically
2. **Source Verification** - Prioritize primary sources (school websites, AO interviews, court documents)
3. **Pattern Recognition** - Identify what's consistent across sources vs. contradictory
4. **Gap Acknowledgment** - Note what couldn't be found for future research
5. **Cross-Section Integration** - Connect findings to related sections
