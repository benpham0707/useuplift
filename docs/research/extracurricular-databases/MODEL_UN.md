# Extracurricular Achievement Database: MODEL UNITED NATIONS (MUN)

**Category:** Academic Competition / International Relations / Public Speaking
**Last Updated:** January 2026
**Usage:** System can query this file specifically for Model UN activity evaluation

---

## Overview

Model United Nations (MUN) is a popular extracurricular that simulates UN proceedings. **Conference prestige and awards matter significantly** - mere participation has limited impact.

> "Participation alone carries modest weight in admissions decisions. Students attending 2-3 conferences annually without leadership roles or awards demonstrate interest but not exceptional achievement."
> — CollegeBase

**Key Insight:** MUN is common enough that **differentiation requires awards at prestigious conferences and/or leadership roles**.

## Award Hierarchy

### Awards Given Per Committee

| Award | Rank | Typical % of Committee |
|-------|------|------------------------|
| **Best Delegate** | 1st | ~2-5% (often 1-2 per committee) |
| **Outstanding Delegate** | 2nd | ~5-10% |
| **Honorable Mention** | 3rd | ~10-15% |
| **Position Paper Award** | Writing | Varies |
| **Verbal Commendation** | Recognition | Varies |

### Award Evaluation Criteria (YMUN Example)

1. **Diplomacy:** Quality of negotiations and collaboration
2. **Speaking:** Clarity, persuasiveness, preparation
3. **Position Knowledge:** Understanding of assigned country/topic
4. **Resolution Writing:** Quality of proposals
5. **Procedure:** Following parliamentary procedure

## What Makes MUN Stand Out

### Differentiating Factors

1. **Award Pattern:** Consistent awards across multiple conferences
2. **Conference Level:** Awards at prestigious (Tier 1-2) conferences
3. **Leadership:** Secretary-General, Head Delegate, club leadership
4. **Growth:** Growing school program, founding new conference
5. **Specialization:** Deep expertise in specific committees (Security Council, crisis)
6. **Mentorship:** Training younger delegates

### Common Weaknesses

- "4 years of MUN" without awards or leadership
- Only attending local conferences
- No progression in roles
- Can't articulate what was learned

## Quick Reference: Tier Summary

### Tier 1 (National/Elite) - Top 1-2%
- Best Delegate at HMUN, NAIMUN, ILMUNC, or HNMUN
- Secretary-General of major conference
- Multiple Best Delegates at competitive conferences
- National recognition (Best Delegate awards)

### Tier 2 (Excellent) - Top 2-10%
- Best Delegate at Tier 2-3 conferences
- Outstanding Delegate at Tier 1 conferences
- Head Delegate with strong team results
- Founded/significantly grew school program
- Conference staff (Director, Crisis Director)

### Tier 3 (Good) - Top 10-30%
- Honorable Mention at competitive conferences
- Best Delegate at local conferences
- Club President with involvement
- Consistent participation with some awards

### Tier 4 (Basic) - Majority
- Conference attendance without awards
- Club membership without leadership
- 1-2 conferences total
- Shows interest but not differentiating

---

## Red Flags

- High hours claimed with no awards ever
- "Secretary-General" title at tiny conference with no context
- Claims multiple years but can't discuss specific committees/topics
- No leadership progression despite 3-4 years
