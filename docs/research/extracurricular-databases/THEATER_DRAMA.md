# Extracurricular Achievement Database: THEATER & DRAMA

**Category:** Performing Arts / Creative Arts
**Last Updated:** January 2026
**Usage:** System can query this file specifically for theater and drama activity evaluation

---

## Overview

Theater and drama activities demonstrate creativity, collaboration, communication skills, and dedication. Unlike STEM competitions with clear rankings, theater evaluation depends heavily on **role size, production quality, competition recognition, and demonstrated growth**.

> "Colleges love to see commitment to extracurriculars, especially in leadership roles. Being a thespian shows dedication, teamwork, and passion for the arts."
> — AdmissionSight

**Key Insight:** Theater is valued both for arts-focused applications AND non-arts majors because it demonstrates collaboration, public speaking, and creative problem-solving.

## International Thespian Society (ITS)

### What It Is

The only honor society for middle and high school drama students, with over 2.4 million inductees since 1929.

- **Parent Organization:** Educational Theatre Association (EdTA)
- **Annual Festival:** International Thespian Festival (ITF) at Indiana University
- **Membership:** Must earn points through participation in school productions

### Achievement Tier Classification

| Tier | Achievement | Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | International Thespian Excellence Award (Thespys®) Finalist | Top performers at ITF | Exceptional |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | ITF Scholarship Winner | Competitive | Exceptional |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | State Thespian Festival Top Award | State-level recognition | Strong |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | International Thespian Festival Participant | Selected for nationals | Strong |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Thespian Troupe Officer (President, VP) | Leadership | Strong |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | State Thespian Festival Participant | Regional recognition | Solid |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | ITS Member with High Point Total | Sustained involvement | Solid |
| **Tier 4 (Basic)** | ITS Induction | Shows commitment | Baseline |

### Thespian Point Levels

| Level | Points | Significance |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| **Thespian** | 10 | Basic induction |
| **Honor Thespian** | 60 | Significant involvement |
| **National Honor Thespian** | 480+ | Exceptional dedication |

### Scholarship Opportunities

- **ITF Scholarships:** $40,000+ awarded annually to Thespys® finalists
- **State Chapter Scholarships:** $200,000+ awarded collectively by EdTA chapters
- **College Auditions:** State festivals offer auditions for college programs

## Jimmy Awards (National High School Musical Theatre Awards)

### What It Is

The "Tony Awards for high school students" - the most prestigious musical theater competition for high schoolers in the United States.

- **Founded:** 2009 by Pittsburgh CLO and Nederlander Alliances
- **Structure:** Regional competitions → Jimmy Awards Finals on Broadway
- **Location:** Final competition held annually in New York City
- **Total Scholarships:** $6 million+ awarded since inception

### Competition Flow

1. **School Production** - Student performs in school musical
2. **Regional Competition** - Best Actor/Actress from region selected
3. **Jimmy Awards Week** - Week-long intensive in NYC
4. **Finals** - Performance on Broadway stage

### Achievement Tier Classification

| Tier | Achievement | Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | Jimmy Awards Winner (Best Actor/Actress) | 2 winners nationally | Exceptional - $25,000 scholarship, Broadway recognition |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | Jimmy Awards Finalist | ~10-15 finalists | Exceptional |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Jimmy Awards Nominee | ~60 nominees from all regions | Strong - competed on Broadway |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Regional Competition Winner | 1-2 per region | Strong |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Regional Competition Participant | Selected by school | Solid |

### Notable Alumni

- **Eva Noblezada** - Tony-nominated for Miss Saigon, Hadestown
- **Reneé Rapp** - Mean Girls on Broadway, pop star
- **Andrew Barth Feldman** - Dear Evan Hansen on Broadway
- **Ryan McCartan** - 2011 Best Actor winner

### College Connections

- Winners eligible for **NYU Tisch New Studio on Broadway** 4-year merit scholarship
- Some recipients selected for **Carnegie Mellon Pre-College Drama** 6-week program
- Direct exposure to Broadway casting directors and college recruiters

## August Wilson New Voices (Monologue Competition)

### What It Is

A national competition for high school students performing monologues from August Wilson's ten-play "Century Cycle" about the African American experience.

- **Founded:** 2007 by Kenny Leon and Todd Kreidler
- **Structure:** Regional (16 cities) → Semifinal → Broadway Finals
- **Final Location:** August Wilson Theatre on Broadway

### Achievement Tier Classification

| Tier | Achievement | Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | National Finals Winner | 1-2 winners | Exceptional |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | National Finals Top 3 | 3 of 16 finalists | Exceptional |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | National Finals Participant | 16 students nationally | Very strong |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Regional Competition Winner | 1-3 per region | Strong |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Regional Finalist | Top performers per region | Solid |

### Notable Alumni

- **Joaquina Kalukango** - First-ever winner (2007), Tony Award winner for Paradise Square

### Competition Cities

Atlanta, Boston, Buffalo, Chicago, Dallas, Greensboro, Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Milwaukee, New Haven, New York, Norfolk, Pittsburgh, Portland, San Diego, Seattle

## National Shakespeare Competition

### What It Is

English-Speaking Union's national competition for high school students performing Shakespeare monologues and sonnets.

- **Structure:** School → Branch → Regional → National Finals
- **Finals Location:** New York City
- **Participants:** Students from all 50 states

### Achievement Tier Classification

| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | National Finalist | ~50 students | Strong |

## What Makes Theater Stand Out

### Differentiating Factors

1. **Competition Recognition** - Jimmy Awards, Thespians, Shakespeare
2. **Lead Role Pattern** - Consistent casting over 4 years
3. **Range** - Musical, dramatic, comedic versatility
4. **Technical Skills** - Acting + dance + voice training
5. **Leadership** - Directing, choreography, stage management
6. **Professional Work** - Paid community/regional theater
7. **Selective Programs** - Summer intensives at top conservatories

### Common Weaknesses

- "4 years of drama" with only ensemble roles
- No competition participation
- Single production type (only musicals OR only plays)
- No leadership or technical experience
- No summer training or intensives

## Quick Reference: Tier Summary

### Tier 1 (National/Elite) - Top 1%

- Jimmy Awards Winner or Finalist
- August Wilson National Finals Winner
- National Shakespeare Competition Champion
- Thespys® Winner at ITF
- Professional regional theater lead
- Acceptance to top summer conservatory (Juilliard, Carnegie Mellon)

### Tier 2 (Excellent) - Top 2-15%

- Jimmy Awards Regional Winner/Nominee
- August Wilson National Finalist
- State Thespian Festival Top Award
- ITF Participant with recognition
- Multiple lead roles in quality program
- Selective summer program participant
- Technical design award winner
- Student director of mainstage production

### Tier 3 (Good) - Top 15-40%

- State Thespian Festival Participant
- Regional competition finalist
- Lead roles in 2-3 productions
- Technical crew head
- ITS member with high points
- Summer program participant

### Tier 4 (Basic) - Majority

- Ensemble/supporting roles only
- Basic Thespian induction
- Crew member without leadership
- No competition participation

## Red Flags

- Claims "lead actor" but can't name productions/roles
- High hours with only ensemble credits
- No progression from supporting to lead over 4 years
- Lists drama club but no actual production credits
- Claims competition without specifying level/result
