# Extracurricular Achievement Database: CREATIVE WRITING

**Category:** Literary Arts / Humanities
**Last Updated:** January 2026
**Usage:** System can query this file specifically for creative writing activity evaluation

---

## Overview

Creative writing achievements demonstrate intellectual depth, communication ability, and artistic talent. These are particularly valued for humanities-focused applicants but also show well-roundedness for STEM applicants.

> "Competitions like YoungArts and the Scholastic Art and Writing Awards have skyrocketed in selectivity and prestige over the past few decades, becoming a quantifiable way for colleges to identify rising literary stars."
> — The Harvard Crimson

**Key Insight:** The creative writing competition space is now highly competitive, with over 340,000 annual submissions to Scholastic alone. National recognition carries significant weight, comparable to STEM competition achievements.

## Scholastic Art & Writing Awards

### What It Is

The nation's longest-running and most prestigious recognition program for creative teenagers, established in 1923.

- **Annual Submissions:** ~340,000 works
- **Categories:** Writing, Art, and Portfolio
- **Process:** Regional → National adjudication
- **Notable Alumni:** Stephen King, Sylvia Plath, Truman Capote, Joyce Carol Oates, John Updike, Langston Hughes

### Writing Categories

- **Short Story**
- **Poetry**
- **Personal Essay/Memoir**
- **Critical Essay**
- **Humor**
- **Journalism**
- **Science Fiction/Fantasy**
- **Flash Fiction**
- **Novel Writing** (Portfolio)
- **Writing Portfolio** (5-8 works)

### Award Levels

| Level | Scope | Significance |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| **National Gold Medal** | National | Highest honor (~500 recipients) |
| **National Silver Medal** | National | High honors (~500 recipients) |
| **American Voices Medal** | National | One per region (best portfolio) |
| **Gold Key** | Regional | Top regional work; advances to national |
| **Silver Key** | Regional | Exceptional regional recognition |
| **Honorable Mention** | Regional | Shows promise |

### Statistics & Selectivity

| Metric | Number |
|--------|--------|
| **Total Submissions** | ~340,000 annually |
| **Gold Key Advances to National** | ~30,000 |
| **National Gold Medals** | ~500 (~1.5% of national entries) |
| **National Silver Medals** | ~500 |
| **Overall National Medal Rate** | <1% of all submissions |

### Achievement Tier Classification

| Tier | Achievement | Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | Portfolio Gold Medal ($10K scholarship) | 16 nationally | Exceptional |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | American Voices Medal | 1 per region | Exceptional |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | National Gold Medal | ~500 of 340,000 (<0.15%) | Exceptional |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | National Silver Medal | ~500 of 340,000 | Very strong |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Multiple Gold Keys | Regional excellence | Strong |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Gold Key (Regional) | Top regional | Solid |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Multiple Silver Keys | Consistent recognition | Solid |
| **Tier 4 (Basic)** | Silver Key or Honorable Mention | Regional recognition | Shows involvement |

### College Admissions Impact

> "Admissions data from multiple institutions indicates that Scholastic National Medalists enjoy acceptance rates 2-3 times higher than general applicant pools at selective colleges. At Brown University, Scholastic National Medalists between 2019-2023 saw acceptance rates of approximately 18% compared to the general rate of 6%."
> — CollegeBase

**Universities Valuing Scholastic Awards:**
- Yale, Harvard, Stanford (cited as "hooks")
- Brown, Columbia, NYU Tisch
- RISD, Parsons, CalArts (for visual arts)
- Northwestern, Emory, Vanderbilt

## YoungArts

### What It Is

National Foundation for Advancement in the Arts program supporting exceptional young artists ages 15-18.

- **Founded:** 1981
- **Disciplines:** 10 artistic disciplines including Writing
- **Writing Genres:** Poetry, Short Story, Novel, Play/Script, Creative Nonfiction, Spoken Word

### Statistics

| Metric | Number |
|--------|--------|
| **Annual Applications** | ~11,000 (record in 2025) |
| **Award Winners Selected** | ~800 |
| **Finalists (With Distinction)** | ~170 |
| **Discipline Winners** | ~20 |
| **Grand Prize Winner** | 1 |

### Award Levels

| Level | Cash Award | Benefits |
|-------|------------|----------|
| **Grand Prize Winner** | Part of $10,000 | Highest honor |
| **Winner (Discipline)** | Up to $10,000 | Top in discipline |
| **Finalist (With Distinction)** | $5,000 | National YoungArts Week invite |
| **Merit** | $1,500 | Recognition |
| **Honorable Mention** | $250 | Recognition |

### Achievement Tier Classification

| Tier | Achievement | Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | Grand Prize Winner | 1 of 11,000 | Exceptional |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | Writing Discipline Winner | ~2 of 11,000 | Exceptional |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | Finalist (With Distinction) | ~170 of 11,000 (~1.5%) | Exceptional |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Merit Award | ~300+ of 11,000 | Strong |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Honorable Mention | ~300+ of 11,000 | Solid |

### Presidential Scholars Pathway

YoungArts is the **exclusive pathway** to U.S. Presidential Scholars in the Arts:
1. YoungArts Finalists attend National YoungArts Week (January)
2. Up to 60 are nominated to Presidential Scholars Commission
3. 20 are selected as U.S. Presidential Scholars in the Arts

> "U.S. Presidential Scholars in the Arts is the nation's highest honor for artistically talented high school seniors."

## Bennington Young Writers Awards

### What It Is

One of the most prestigious high school writing competitions, hosted by Bennington College.

- **Eligibility:** Grades 9-12 worldwide
- **Categories:** Poetry, Fiction, Nonfiction
- **Deadline:** November 1 annually
- **Cost:** Free to enter

### Statistics

| Metric | Number |
|--------|--------|
| **Annual Submissions** | 5,800-7,000 |
| **Winners Selected** | 9 (3 per category) |
| **Win Rate** | ~0.1-0.15% |

### Prizes

| Place | Cash Prize | If Enrolled at Bennington |
|-------|------------|---------------------------|
| **1st Place** | $2,000 | $60,000 scholarship (4 years) |
| **2nd Place** | $1,000 | $40,000 scholarship (4 years) |
| **3rd Place** | $500 | $40,000 scholarship (4 years) |
| **Finalist** | - | $40,000 scholarship (4 years) |

### Achievement Tier Classification

| Tier | Achievement | Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | 1st Place Winner | 3 of ~6,500 (~0.05%) | Exceptional |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | 2nd or 3rd Place | 6 of ~6,500 (~0.1%) | Very strong |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Finalist | Selected from thousands | Strong |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Honorable Mention | Top tier recognition | Solid |

### Notable Past Winners

## The Adroit Prizes

### What It Is

Highly respected literary competition run by The Adroit Journal, known for showcasing emerging voices alongside established authors.

- **Categories:** Poetry and Prose (fiction/creative nonfiction)
- **Eligibility:** High school and college students globally
- **Poetry:** Up to 5 poems
- **Prose:** Up to 3 pieces (combined 3,500 words max)

### Prizes

- **Winners:** $200 + publication in Adroit Journal
- **All Entries:** Considered for publication

### Achievement Tier Classification

| Tier | Achievement | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Prize Winner | Strong - prestigious publication |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Published in Adroit (non-prize) | Solid |

## The Concord Review

### What It Is

The only academic journal in the world publishing high school students' history research papers.

- **Founded:** 1987
- **Focus:** History research (5,000-21,000+ words)
- **Format:** Turabian/Chicago citation style
- **Cost:** $70-150 submission fee

See **STEM_RESEARCH.md** for detailed Concord Review information.

### Quick Reference

| Metric | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Acceptance Rate** | 5-8% (~45 of ~900) |
| **Tier** | Tier 2 (Excellent) - "Like winning a national math contest" per Harvard Dean |

## What Makes Writing Stand Out

### Differentiating Factors

1. **National Recognition** - Scholastic Gold, YoungArts, Bennington
2. **Publication** - In respected literary magazines
3. **Sustained Excellence** - Multiple awards over years
4. **Range** - Poetry AND prose achievement
5. **Leadership** - Founded magazine, led workshops
6. **Selective Programs** - Iowa, Kenyon, Sewanee acceptance

### Common Weaknesses

- Only local/school-level recognition
- Self-published work without external validation
- Claims "writer" without tangible output
- No competition participation despite years of writing

---

## Red Flags

- Claims publication without naming the venue
- Lists writing as passion but has no competition entries
- "Published author" via vanity/pay-to-publish press
- Cannot share work samples when asked
- Vague descriptions ("won writing awards") without specifics
