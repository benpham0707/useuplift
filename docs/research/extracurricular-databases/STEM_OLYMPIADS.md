## Overview: STEM Olympiads in College Admissions

### Why Olympiads Matter

| Factor | Significance |
|--------|--------------|
| **National team** | Major hook at any school |
| **National finalist** | Strong differentiator |
| **State/regional level** | Solid achievement |
| **Participation** | Shows interest, limited distinction |

> "An olympiad medalist shows intellectual depth in a way that's hard to replicate. It's not just about the medal—it's about the problem-solving ability and dedication required to get there."
> — MIT Admissions Officer

### Olympiad Hierarchy

**Tier 1 (Elite/National):**
- National team member
- Camp qualifier (USAMO, USAPhO camp, etc.)
- Top 20-50 nationally

**Tier 2 (Strong):**
- National exam qualifier (USAMO, USAPHO, etc.)
- Top 100-500 nationally
- State champion

**Tier 3 (Good):**
- Qualifier for state/regional finals
- Top score at school
- Solid performance at first-round exam

**Tier 4 (Participation):**
- Took first-round exam
- Participated but didn't advance

## Mathematics Olympiads

### AMC/AIME/USA(J)MO Pathway

**The primary mathematics competition pathway in the US:**

```
AMC 8 (Middle School)
    ↓
AMC 10/12 (High School)
    ↓
AIME (American Invitational Math Exam)
    ↓
USA(J)MO (USA Math Olympiad / USA Junior Math Olympiad)
    ↓
MOP (Math Olympiad Program / Summer Camp)
    ↓
IMO Team Selection (6 students)
```

### AMC Achievement Tiers

| Exam | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| **AMC 8** | Perfect (25) | Distinguished Honor Roll (DHR: top 1%) | Honor Roll (top 5%) | Participation |
| **AMC 10** | Perfect or 144+ (AIME floor) | 120+ | 100+ | Participation |
| **AMC 12** | Perfect or 136.5+ (AIME floor) | 100+ | 80+ | Participation |

**Note:** AIME qualification cutoffs vary yearly; these are approximate.

### AIME Achievement Tiers

| Achievement | Tier | Approximate Level |
|-------------|------|-------------------|

| **AIME 10+** | Tier 1-2 | USA(J)MO range |

| **AIME 4-6** | Tier 2-3 | Solid performance |
| **AIME 1-3** | Tier 3 | Qualified but struggled |

## Cross-Olympiad Strategy

### Depth vs. Breadth

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **Focus on one olympiad** | Maximizes achievement level | All eggs in one basket |
| **Two related olympiads** | Shows range within field | May dilute top performance |
| **Many olympiads at lower level** | Shows broad interest | Looks unfocused |

**Best practice:** Go deep in one or two related olympiads rather than shallow in many.

### Building an Olympiad Profile

| Grade | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **Freshman** | Explore; take first exams; join math circle |
| **Sophomore** | Commit to 1-2 olympiads; serious study; first camp applications |
| **Junior** | Peak achievement year; qualify for national exams/camps |
| **Senior** | Maintain or improve; don't abandon |

### Time Investment

| Achievement Level | Typical Hours/Week | Preparation |
|-------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **National team** | 15-25 hrs/week | Years of intensive study |
| **National finalist** | 10-15 hrs/week | Consistent study over 2-3 years |
| **State/regional** | 5-10 hrs/week | Regular practice |
| **Participation** | 1-5 hrs/week | Basic preparation |

## Olympiad + Research Combination

**The strongest STEM profiles often combine:**

1. **Olympiad achievement** - Shows problem-solving ability
2. **Research** - Shows ability to generate new knowledge
3. **Application/Creation** - Shows real-world implementation

**Example strong profile:**
- USAMO Qualifier (math olympiad)
- Regeneron Science Talent Search Top 300 (research)
- Published app or open-source project (creation)

## Common Progression Indicators

### Signs of Future Success

| Signal | What It Predicts |
|--------|------------------|
| **AMC 8 Distinguished Honor Roll** | Strong potential for later olympiads |
| **MATHCOUNTS State finalist** | Good AIME trajectory |
| **Freshman AIME qualifier** | Strong USAMO potential |
| **Early promotion in USACO** | Good trajectory |
| **Science Olympiad event medals as underclassman** | Leadership and depth potential |

### Red Flags

| Red Flag | Concern |
|----------|---------|
| **Claims qualification without verification** | Easy to check |
| **Vague about scores or levels** | May not have achieved what they claim |
| **No improvement over time** | Stagnation |
| **Only participates, never advances** | Limited achievement |
| **Can't discuss problem-solving approaches** | May not have done the work |
