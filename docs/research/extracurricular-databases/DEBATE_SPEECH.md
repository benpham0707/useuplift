## Overview

Debate and speech are among the most valued extracurriculars at elite colleges because they directly demonstrate:
- Critical thinking and argumentation
- Research skills
- Communication abilities
- Competitive achievement under pressure

**Two Major Competition Tracks:**
1. **National Circuit (TOC)** - Most prestigious, invitation-only
2. **NSDA (National Speech & Debate Association)** - Largest, most accessible

---

## Tournament of Champions (TOC)

### What It Is

> "The Tournament of Champions is considered to be the national championship of the 'National Circuit,' and is one of the most prestigious and competitive American high school speech and debate tournaments."
> — Wikipedia

- **Founded:** 1972
- **Location:** University of Kentucky (annual, April)
- **Status:** Most rigorous and prestigious high school debate competition globally

### Qualification System

**Bid System:**
- Requires **2 Gold bids** for Gold Division
- Requires **2 Silver bids OR 1 Gold bid** for Silver Division
- Silver bids awarded one elimination round before gold bid threshold

**How Bids Work:**
- Over 100 qualifying tournaments nationwide
- Place high enough at "bid tournaments" to earn bids
- Popular bid tournaments: Greenhill (TX), Harvard (MA), UC Berkeley (CA)

**Selectivity:**
- ~2,000 students qualify for TOC bids annually (all events)
- Fewer than 500 ultimately compete at TOC
- ~15-20% qualify through at-large bids

## NSDA (National Speech & Debate Association)

### What It Is

- **Formerly:** National Forensic League (NFL), founded 1925
- **Membership:** ~140,000 student members nationwide
- **Structure:** 110+ districts across the country
- **Status:** Largest interscholastic speech/debate organization

### Competition Structure

| Level | Description | Advancement |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Local Tournaments** | School/league competitions | Build experience |
| **District Tournament** | Annual qualifying competition | Top performers advance to Nationals |
| **National Tournament** | Annual championship | ~6,000 students from 1,200 schools |

### District Qualification

- Top 14 competitors in main events per district advance
- Top 6 in supplemental events advance
- Requires 25+ NSDA merit points (Degree of Merit)

### NSDA Events

**Main Events:**
- Lincoln-Douglas Debate (LD)
- Policy Debate (CX)
- Public Forum Debate (PF)
- Congressional Debate
- Original Oratory
- Dramatic Interpretation
- Humorous Interpretation
- Duo Interpretation
- Extemporaneous Speaking

**Supplemental Events:**
- Informative Speaking
- Program Oral Interpretation
- Storytelling
- Big Questions Debate

### NSDA Achievement Tiers

| Tier | Achievement | Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | NSDA Nationals Champion | 1 per event | Exceptional |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | NSDA Nationals Finalist | Top 2 per event | Exceptional |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | NSDA National Student of the Year | 1 student | Exceptional individual honor |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | NSDA Nationals Semifinalist | ~4-8 per event | Very strong |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | NSDA Nationals Octofinalist | Elimination rounds | Strong |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | District Student of the Year | 1 per district | Strong leadership recognition |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Academic All-American | Top academics + competition | Strong |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | NSDA Nationals Qualifier | Top 14 per district (~6,000 total) | Solid - top of district |

| **Tier 4 (Basic)** | NSDA member with points | 140,000 members | Baseline participation |

### State Tournament Achievement

| Achievement | Typical Selectivity | Tier |
|-------------|---------------------|------|
| **State Champion** | 1 per event | Tier 2 |
| **State Finalist** | Top 2-6 | Tier 2-3 |
| **State Qualifier** | Varies (10-30%) | Tier 3 |

## Quick Reference: Tier Summary

### Tier 1 (National/Elite) - Top 0.1-1%
- TOC Champion/Finalist/Semifinalist
- NSDA Nationals Champion/Finalist
- NSDA National Student of the Year
- Multiple Gold TOC bids with deep elimination rounds

### Tier 2 (State+/Excellent) - Top 1-10%
- TOC Qualifier (earned 2+ bids)
- NSDA Nationals Octofinalist+
- State Champion
- District Student of the Year
- Academic All-American
- Gold bid winner at major tournament

### Tier 3 (Regional/Good) - Top 10-30%
- NSDA Nationals Qualifier
- State Finalist/Semifinalist
- Silver bid winner
- Team Captain with results
- High NSDA point totals (500+)

### Tier 4 (Participation/Basic) - Majority
- Local tournament participation
- NSDA member with basic points
- JV/Novice debater
- Shows interest but not differentiating

## Red Flags

- Claims leadership but no competitive achievement
- Inflated hours without corresponding success
