# Extracurricular Achievement Database Index

**Purpose:** This directory contains detailed, research-backed databases for evaluating specific extracurricular activities in college admissions contexts.

**System Usage:** The system can query individual files based on the activity type being evaluated, avoiding the need to load all databases simultaneously.

## Tier System Reference

All databases use a consistent 4-tier achievement classification:

| Tier | Level | Typical Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1** | National/Elite | Top 0.1-2% | Exceptional - can be primary hook |
| **Tier 2** | State+/Excellent | Top 2-15% | Strong - significant differentiator |
| **Tier 3** | Regional/Good | Top 15-40% | Solid - meaningful contribution |
| **Tier 4** | Participation/Basic | Majority | Baseline - shows interest only |

## Quick Lookup by Activity

### When evaluating a student's activity, find the right database:

**"I did robotics"** → [ROBOTICS.md](./ROBOTICS.md)
- Look for: FRC, VEX, FTC level and awards

**"I did debate"** → [DEBATE_SPEECH.md](./DEBATE_SPEECH.md)
- Look for: TOC bids, NSDA nationals, state rankings

**"I did Model UN"** → [MODEL_UN.md](./MODEL_UN.md)

**"I did research"** → [STEM_RESEARCH.md](./STEM_RESEARCH.md)
- Look for: Competition results, publications, summer programs

**"I did math/physics/chem/bio olympiad"** → [STEM_OLYMPIADS.md](./STEM_OLYMPIADS.md)
- Look for: Division level, camp invitations, national team

**"I did USACO"** → [STEM_OLYMPIADS.md](./STEM_OLYMPIADS.md) or [HACKATHONS_CS.md](./HACKATHONS_CS.md)
- Look for: Division (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum), camp invitation

- Look for: Jimmy Awards, Thespians, lead roles, technical awards

**"I write"** → [CREATIVE_WRITING.md](./CREATIVE_WRITING.md)
- Look for: Scholastic medals, YoungArts, publications

**"I do visual art"** → [VISUAL_ARTS.md](./VISUAL_ARTS.md)
- Look for: Scholastic medals, YoungArts, portfolio quality, exhibitions

**"I play music"** → [MUSIC.md](./MUSIC.md)
- Look for: YoungArts, All-State/All-National, competition placement, conservatory prep

**"I play sports"** → [ATHLETICS.md](./ATHLETICS.md)
- Look for: Recruitment status, All-State, team achievements, division level

**"I started a business"** → [ENTREPRENEURSHIP.md](./ENTREPRENEURSHIP.md)
- Look for: DECA/FBLA awards, revenue, user metrics

**"I did community service"** → [COMMUNITY_SERVICE.md](./COMMUNITY_SERVICE.md)

## Universal Red Flags (All Activities)

1. **Vague descriptions** - Cannot specify awards, roles, or outcomes
2. **No progression** - Same level for 4 years
3. **Hour inflation** - High hours with no corresponding achievements
4. **Missing verification** - No website, records, or documentation
5. **Started senior year** - Lacks sustained commitment
6. **Cannot explain** - Doesn't understand what they supposedly did
7. **Derivative work claimed as original** - Applies to creative fields
8. **Recruitment claims without coach contact** - Applies to athletics
9. **Competition claims that don't exist** - Easy to verify against public records

## Usage Example

**Student says:** "I won Best Delegate at HMUN and got silver at NAIMUN"

**System lookup:**
1. Identify activity: Model UN → Load [MODEL_UN.md](./MODEL_UN.md)
2. Check conference tier: HMUN = Tier 1, NAIMUN = Tier 1
3. Check award level: Best Delegate = Top 2-5% per committee
4. Assessment: **Tier 1 (Elite)** - Best Delegate at Tier 1 conference + strong pattern

**Output:** "Outstanding MUN achievement - Best Delegate at two of the most prestigious conferences (HMUN and NAIMUN) represents exceptional performance, comparable to debate TOC or science olympiad national medals."

---

**Student says:** "I made USACO Gold and got All-State in swimming"

**System lookup:**
1. Identify activities: USACO → [STEM_OLYMPIADS.md](./STEM_OLYMPIADS.md); Swimming → [ATHLETICS.md](./ATHLETICS.md)
2. Check USACO Gold: Tier 2 (top ~500-1000 students nationally)
3. Check All-State swimming: Tier 2-3 (depending on state and times)
4. Cross-reference: Different fields; suggests breadth with depth in CS

**Output:** "Strong profile showing depth in competitive programming (USACO Gold = Tier 2, top ~500 nationally) combined with athletic commitment (All-State swimming). The CS achievement is the stronger differentiator; swimming shows balance and discipline."

---

**Student says:** "I play violin and made All-State"

**System lookup:**
1. Identify activity: Music/Violin → Load [MUSIC.md](./MUSIC.md)
2. Check All-State: Tier 2-3 depending on position (principal vs. section)
3. Check for additional context: Other competitions, summer programs, lessons

**Output:** "All-State violin is a solid Tier 2-3 achievement depending on position (principal chair is stronger). To strengthen this profile, consider: YoungArts application, regional/national competitions, summer programs like Tanglewood BUTI or Meadowmount. For conservatory track, additional competition placement is important; for non-music majors, All-State is a strong supporting activity."
