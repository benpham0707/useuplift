# Extracurricular Achievement Database: ROBOTICS

**Category:** STEM Competition / Engineering
**Last Updated:** January 2026
**Usage:** System can query this file specifically for robotics-related activity evaluation

---

## Overview

Robotics competitions are among the most common STEM extracurriculars, with over 100,000 students participating in FRC alone annually. Because of this ubiquity, **mere participation is NOT differentiating** - achievement level matters significantly.

> "FRC isn't going to be the one thing that makes you unique, or even rare, to an admissions committee. It's something that's on the resumes of a lot of students applying for STEM programs."
> — Chief Delphi Forum

## FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC)

### Competition Structure

| Level | Description | Scale |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| **Regional/District Events** | First level of competition | 98 District + 62 Regional events (2024) |
| **District Championship** | Top district teams advance | 11 District Championships |
| **World Championship** | Top teams from all regions | ~600 teams qualify |
| **Division Play** | 8 divisions at Worlds | Named after STEM figures |
| **Einstein Field** | Division winners compete | Final tournament |
| **FIRST Champion** | Winning alliance on Einstein | 4 teams annually |

**Total Participants:** ~100,000 students on ~3,500 teams (2024)

### Achievement Tier Classification

| Tier | Achievement | Selectivity | Admissions Impact |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | Einstein Field Winner/Finalist | Top 8-16 teams of 3,500 (~0.2-0.5%) | Exceptional - equivalent to national olympiad |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | FIRST Dean's List Award | 2 nominees per team, ~150 finalists | Exceptional individual recognition |
| **Tier 1 (Elite)** | Impact Award Winner | 1 per division + Einstein | Equivalent to FIRST "Nobel Prize" |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | World Championship Division Winner | Top 8 alliances of 600+ | Very strong - national-level |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | World Championship Qualifier | ~600 of 3,500 teams (~17%) | Strong achievement |
| **Tier 2 (Excellent)** | Regional/District Event Winner | ~200-300 teams | Strong regional achievement |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Regional/District Event Finalist | Top 8 alliances per event | Solid achievement |
| **Tier 3 (Good)** | Team Leadership Role | Captain, President, Lead | Shows responsibility |
| **Tier 4 (Basic)** | Team Member/Participant | 100,000 students | Baseline - not differentiating |

### Individual Awards (FRC)

| Award | Description | Selectivity | Impact |
|-------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **FIRST Dean's List** | Top student leaders | 2 per team eligible, ~150 finalists nationally | Tier 1 - highest individual honor |
| **Woodie Flowers Finalist** | Mentor recognition | N/A for students | N/A |
| **Impact Award** | Best embodiment of FIRST mission | 1 per Championship division | Tier 1 for team |
| **Engineering Inspiration** | Spreading STEM awareness | Per regional/division | Tier 2 |
| **Rookie All-Star** | Best first-year team | Per regional | Tier 2-3 |
| **Chairman's Award** (now Impact) | Legacy highest honor | Was most prestigious | Tier 1 |

### Leadership Roles Evaluation

| Role | Hours Expected | Admissions Weight |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Team Captain/President** | 15-25 hrs/week in season | Strong leadership signal |
| **Subteam Lead** (Programming, Mechanical, etc.) | 10-20 hrs/week | Technical depth |
| **Strategy Lead** | 10-15 hrs/week | Analytical skills |
| **Outreach Coordinator** | 5-15 hrs/week | Community engagement |
| **General Member** | 5-15 hrs/week | Participation only |

### College Recognition

> "Stanford and Northwestern admissions have highlighted FRC success as evidence of 'collaborative excellence' in admitted student profiles."
> — CollegeBase

**Universities with Known FRC Appreciation:**
- MIT (many students come from FRC)
- Stanford (values "collaborative excellence")
- Northwestern (engineering emphasis)
- Harvey Mudd
- Carnegie Mellon
- Georgia Tech
- Purdue
- Michigan

**Research-Backed Impact (Brandeis University Study):**
- FRC students 2-3x more likely to improve STEM skills
- 81% of FIRST alumni choose STEM majors by 4th year
- 90% report gaining teamwork skills
- 85% report improved problem-solving

## Cross-Cutting Evaluation Criteria

### What Makes Robotics Stand Out (Beyond Participation)

1. **Competition Achievement** - Wins, advancement, awards
2. **Leadership Role** - Captain, lead, coordinator
3. **Technical Depth** - Can explain engineering decisions
4. **Longevity** - 3-4 years of involvement
5. **Quantified Impact** - "Grew team from 10 to 25 members"
6. **Outreach** - Teaching others, community demos
7. **Unique Contribution** - "Designed autonomous navigation system"

### Red Flags

- "Member of robotics team" with no other details
- High hours claimed without leadership or achievement
- Robotics only activity (no technical depth visible elsewhere)
- Multiple robotics teams with shallow involvement in each

### Activity Description Examples

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Member of robotics team" | "Led 5-person programming subteam; developed autonomous navigation; regional finalists" |
| "Participated in FRC" | "Captain of 25-member FRC team; qualified for World Championship; raised $15K in sponsorships" |
| "VEX robotics" | "State champion (VEX); designed award-winning drivetrain; mentored 3 rookie teams" |

## Quick Reference: Tier Summary

### Tier 1 (National/Exceptional) - Top 0.5-2%
- FRC: Einstein Field, Dean's List, Impact Award
- VEX: World Championship Winner/Finalist, Excellence Award (Worlds)
- FTC: World Championship Winner, Inspire Award
- Any: BattleBots TV competitor, MATE International Winner

### Tier 2 (State/Excellent) - Top 2-15%
- FRC: World Championship Qualifier, Regional Winner
- VEX: World Championship Qualifier, State Champion
- FTC: World Championship Qualifier, State Champion
- Any: Significant leadership role + strong results

### Tier 3 (Regional/Good) - Top 15-40%
- FRC: Regional Finalist, Subteam Lead
- VEX: State Qualifier, Tournament Winner
- FTC: State Qualifier
- Any: Active member with some distinction

### Tier 4 (Participation/Basic) - Majority
- General team membership without distinction
- Shows STEM interest but not differentiating
