2025-2026 Supplemental Essay Requirements for Top 30 U.S. Universities
Executive Summary
This comprehensive report details the supplemental essay requirements for the top 30 universities in the United States for the 2025-2026 application cycle. All applications are due within approximately one month (early December 2025 to early January 2026). The report identifies duplicate essay prompts across institutions and provides complete, up-to-date information verified through official university sources and admissions platforms.

Key Findings:

All top 30 universities require supplemental essays beyond the Common Application personal statement

Total essay requirements range from 1-8 supplemental essays per institution

Common deadlines: November 1 (Early Decision/Action), January 1-15 (Regular Decision), December 1 (UC schools)

Most common essay types: "Why This School?", "Why This Major?", Community/Diversity, and Activity-based prompts

Complete List of Top 30 Universities and Their Supplemental Essays
1. Princeton University​
Deadlines:

Single-Choice Early Action: November 1, 2025

Regular Decision: January 1, 2026

Required Supplemental Essays:

Academic Interest Essay (250 words) - Choose based on intended degree

For A.B./Undecided applicants: "As a research institution that also prides itself on its liberal arts curriculum, Princeton allows students to explore areas across the humanities and the arts, the natural sciences, and the social sciences. What academic areas most pique your curiosity, and how do the programs offered at Princeton suit your particular interests?"

For B.S.E. applicants: "Please describe why you are interested in studying engineering at Princeton. Include any of your experiences in or exposure to engineering, and how you think the programs offered at the University suit your particular interests."

Community & Experience Essay (500 words)

"Princeton has a longstanding commitment to understanding our responsibility to society through service and civic engagement. How does your own story intersect with these ideals?"

Short Answer Questions:

Question 1 (50 words): "What is a new skill you would like to learn in college?"

Question 2 (50 words): "What brings you joy?"

Question 3 (50 words): "What is one thing you love about where you come from?"

Additional Requirement: One graded written paper from high school

🔁 DUPLICATE ALERT: The "Why This School/Major" structure is similar to other top schools, but Princeton's specific prompts are unique.

2. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)​
Deadlines:

Early Action: November 1, 2025

Regular Decision: January 1, 2026

Required Supplemental Essays: (100-200 words each)

Academic Interest Essay

"What field of study appeals to you the most right now? Tell us more about why this field of study at MIT appeals to you."

Personal Interest Essay

"We know you lead a busy life, full of activities, many of which are required of you. Tell us about something you do simply for the pleasure of it."

Educational Journey Essay

"While some reach their goals following well-trodden paths, others blaze their own trails achieving the unexpected. In what ways have you done something different than what was expected in your educational journey?"

Collaboration Essay

"MIT brings people with diverse backgrounds together to collaborate, from tackling the world's biggest challenges to lending a helping hand. Describe one way you have collaborated with others to learn from them, with them, or contribute to your community together."

Challenge Essay

"How did you manage a situation or challenge that you didn't expect? What did you learn from it?"

Activities List (40 words per activity)

"Please list up to four activities—if you have more than four, choose the ones that are most important to you."

Optional Additional Information (650 words)

"There is also one final, open-ended, additional-information text box where you can tell us anything else you think we really ought to know."

🔁 DUPLICATE ALERT: The "collaboration" and "challenge" prompts appear in similar forms at Yale, Harvard, Columbia, and Brown.

8. Northwestern University​
Deadlines:

Early Decision/Action: November 1, 2025

Regular Decision: January 2, 2026

Required Supplemental Essay:

Context & Community Essay (300 words or fewer)

"We want to be sure we're considering your application in the context of your personal experiences: What aspects of your background (your identity, your school setting, your community, your household, etc.) have most shaped how you see yourself engaging in Northwestern's community, be it academically, extracurricularly, culturally, politically, socially, or otherwise?"

Optional Essays (choose 1-2, 200 words or fewer each):

Option 1: "Painting 'The Rock' is a tradition at Northwestern that invites all forms of expression—students promote campus events or extracurricular groups, support social or activist causes, show their Wildcat spirit (what we call "Purple Pride"), celebrate their culture, and more. What would you paint on The Rock, and why?"

Option 2: "Northwestern fosters a distinctively interdisciplinary culture. We believe discovery and innovation thrive at the intersection of diverse ideas, perspectives, and academic interests. Within this setting, if you could dream up an undergraduate class, research project, or creative effort (a start-up, a design prototype, a performance, etc.), what would it be? Who might be some ideal classmates or collaborators?"

Option 3: "Community and belonging matter at Northwestern. Tell us about one or more communities, networks, or student groups you see yourself connecting with on campus."

Option 4: "Northwestern's location is special: on the shore of Lake Michigan, steps from downtown Evanston, just a few miles from Chicago. What aspects of our location are most compelling to you, and why?"

Option 5: "Northwestern is a place where people with diverse backgrounds from all over the world can study, live, and talk with one another. This range of experiences and viewpoints immeasurably enriches learning. How might your individual background contribute to this diversity of perspectives in Northwestern's classrooms and around our campus?"

🔁 DUPLICATE ALERT:

The "background/context" required essay is very similar to UMich's leadership essay and many diversity essays

20. University of California, Berkeley (UC Berkeley)​

UC Personal Insight Questions:

All UC schools (Berkeley, UCLA, San Diego, Davis, Irvine, Santa Barbara, Santa Cruz, Riverside, Merced) use the SAME essay prompts. Applicants must answer 4 out of 8 questions, 350 words each.

Community Improvement: "What have you done to make your school or your community a better place?"

Beyond the Application: "Beyond what has already been shared in your application, what do you believe makes you a strong candidate for admissions to the University of California?"
