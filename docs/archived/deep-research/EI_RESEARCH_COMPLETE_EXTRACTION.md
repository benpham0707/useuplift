## Neuroscience Weight Justifications

### Why These Weights?

1. **Emotional Maturity (10%)**
   - Adolescent prefrontal cortex still developing [^6]
   - Demonstrated maturity is developmentally significant
   - Predicts college readiness better than academics alone [^5]

2. **Authentic Vulnerability (8%)**
   - Oxytocin boost of 47% from emotional engagement [^68]
   - Mirror neurons create reader identification [^71]
   - BUT: Performed vulnerability detected, backfires [^16]

3. **Empathy Demonstration (8%)**
   - Theory of mind regions activated by narratives [^67]
   - Predicts collaborative success in college [^7]
   - BUT: Savior framing backfires [^41][^44]

4. **Self-Awareness (10%)**
   - Harvard explicitly lists as key attribute [^3]
   - Correlates with growth potential [^49]
   - Must include weaknesses, not just strengths [^46]

5. **Emotional Complexity (6%)**
   - Signals sophistication vs. immaturity [^56]
   - Ongoing struggles more authentic than resolution [^2]
   - BUT: Not chaos - must be coherent
