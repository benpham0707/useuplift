## UX Principles Analysis

### 1. No Preemptive Warnings

> **Principle:** The AI should not warn students about mistakes before they make them.
> Warnings should only appear when the AI observes an issue in the student's writing.

**Status:** ✅ PASS

### 2. Probing Questions

> **Principle:** The AI should ask questions to draw out specific details from students,
> not just lecture or provide information.

**Status:** ⚠️ PARTIAL

### 3. References Student's Specific Details

> **Principle:** When students share details, the AI should reference them back,
> showing it's listening and building on their input.

**Status:** ⚠️ PARTIAL

### 4. Reactive Coaching

> **Principle:** Common mistakes and warnings should only surface when the AI
> observes the student actually making them, not preemptively.

**Status:** ✅ PASS
