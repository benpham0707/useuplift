## KEY RISKS & MITIGATIONS

### Risk 1: Price Resistance
- **Mitigation**: Free tier proves value, compare to $2K counselors
- **Mitigation**: Monthly payments ($129 vs $299 upfront)

### Risk 2: API Cost Changes
- **Current margin**: 98.8%
- **If Claude raises 50%**: Still 98.2% margin
- **Mitigation**: Lock annual prices, adjust yearly

### Risk 3: Competition
- **Moat**: 50-college research database (3 live, 47 roadmap)
- **Moat**: Citation system (backend complete)
- **Moat**: 4-stage methodology (unique)
- **Moat**: Quality reputation (student testimonials)

### Risk 4: Seasonality
- **Peak**: Sep-Jan (application season)
- **Valley**: Feb-Aug
- **Mitigation**: International markets, year-round content (PIQ, extracurriculars)
