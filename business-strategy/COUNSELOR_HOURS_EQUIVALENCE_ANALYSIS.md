## Methodology: How We Calculate Equivalence

### What Happens in Each Uplift Interaction:

#### 1. Essay Review (6 credits each)
Each essay review provides:
- **12-dimension rubric analysis** (narrative arc, authenticity, voice, specificity, etc.)
- **Detailed feedback** on strengths and weaknesses
- **Specific improvement suggestions** with teaching
- **Original examples** demonstrating techniques
- **Score tracking** showing progress over revisions

**Counselor Equivalent**: A thorough essay review with written feedback from a professional counselor typically takes **15-30 minutes of focused work**, plus **15-30 minutes of discussion** with the student.

→ **1 Uplift essay review ≈ 0.25-0.5 counselor hours**

#### 2. Chat Message (1 credit each)
Each chat interaction provides:
- **Socratic coaching questions** to develop thinking
- **Teaching moments** about writing techniques
- **Clarifying questions** about the student's story
- **Strategy guidance** on essay approach
- **Encouragement and direction**

**Counselor Equivalent**: Each meaningful coaching exchange in a counseling session represents approximately **2-5 minutes** of back-and-forth dialogue.

→ **1 Uplift chat ≈ 0.03-0.08 counselor hours** (2-5 minutes)
