## Northwestern's Essay Philosophy: "Connections" and Predictive Assessment

### Essays Rated "Very Important" - Equal to Core Academics

Northwestern rates essays as **"Very Important"** - equal to rigor/GPA/rank/recommendations/activities/talent/character. Some analyses suggest essays weighted **MORE than extracurriculars**.

**Major Strategic Shift (2023-24)**: Northwestern **eliminated Common App essay requirement**, focusing ONLY on targeted supplemental essays. This signals Northwestern values **purpose-driven, specific responses** over general narratives.

---

### Liz Kinsley's Three Assessment Criteria (Current Director)

> "Our writing supplements are designed to support students by helping them focus their responses on areas we consider most important to our holistic review: **(1) how their personal experiences have shaped various ways they see themselves engaging at Northwestern**, and **(2) how their vision for college aligns with Northwestern's institutional values, academic culture, and campus community**."

**Translation**:
1. **Background → Engagement Connection**: How experiences shape HOW you'll engage (predictive)
2. **Vision Alignment**: Your college vision matching Northwestern's specific culture
3. **Cultural Fit**: Understanding Northwestern's distinctive values (collaboration, interdisciplinary, connections, social responsibility)

---

### "Northwestern is All About Connections"

**FROM ADMISSIONS BLOG**: "We like to say Northwestern is all about **connections**: the relationships people build here, the links we draw between in-class material and real-world settings around us, **you're connecting the dots between your experiences** and **how you envision yourself thriving in college**."

**What This Means**:
- **Relationship-building**: Collaborative mindset, not lone wolf
- **Interdisciplinary thinking**: Drawing links between fields (dedicated essay prompt for this)
- **Theory to practice**: Classroom learning → real-world application
- **Past to future**: Linear connection from experiences → Northwestern vision

## Essay 1: Background & Engagement (300w) - REQUIRED

**Prompt**: "What aspects of your background have most shaped how you see yourself engaging in Northwestern's community?"

**Purpose**: Response to Supreme Court ruling (allows discussion of background/identity) while assessing future engagement prediction.

### Scoring Rubric

**90-100 (Excellent)**:
- Focuses on 1-2 specific background aspects (depth > breadth)
- Shows HOW these shaped perspective/values/approach (not just what they are)
- **LINEAR CONNECTION** to specific Northwestern engagement
- Names 2-3 specific Northwestern opportunities (programs, courses, clubs, traditions)
- Demonstrates research depth (beyond website homepage)
- Shows Northwestern values alignment (collaboration, interdisciplinary, curiosity)
- Balances: ~100w background context, ~200w Northwestern engagement
- Creates predictive case admissions can see

**70-89 (Good)**: Strong but may lack specificity or linear connection depth

**50-69 (Average)**: Generic Northwestern references, weak connection, mostly background without engagement

**Below 50 (Weak)**: All background with zero Northwestern content, or completely generic

## Optional Essays (Choose 2, 200w each)

### Essay 2A: The Rock

**Prompt**: "What would you paint on The Rock, and why?"

**Purpose**: Tests values, passion, creativity, cultural contribution through Northwestern-specific tradition.

**90-100**: Specific painting + deep personal significance + connection to values + how contributes to Northwestern community

**Key Criteria**: (1) Both WHAT and WHY (personal meaning), (2) Reflects authentic values/identity, (3) Shows community contribution mindset, (4) Creative and specific

---

### Essay 2B: Interdisciplinary Class/Project

**Prompt**: "Dream up an undergraduate class, research project, or creative effort. What would it be? Who might be collaborators?"

**Purpose**: Tests Northwestern's **signature value** - interdisciplinary thinking and collaboration.

**90-100**: Topic at intersection of 2+ disciplines + specific synthesis + named collaborators + demonstrates genuine interdisciplinary curiosity

**Key Criteria**: (1) TRUE interdisciplinary (not forced), (2) Specific enough but not too narrow, (3) Shows collaboration, (4) Demonstrates intellectual curiosity

**Red Flag**: Topic already offered or single discipline only

---

### Essay 2C: Academic/Extracurricular Community

**Prompt**: "Which Northwestern co-curricular opportunities might influence your intellectual journey?"

**Purpose**: Tests research depth and understanding that extracurricular→intellectual growth.

**90-100**: 1-2 specific clubs/organizations by name + what they do + HOW they influence INTELLECTUAL journey (not just fun)

**Key Criteria**: (1) Specific Northwestern groups named, (2) Connection to intellectual/academic growth, (3) Research evident, (4) Forward-looking

**Red Flag**: Generic "cultural club" without names; no intellectual connection; lists many superficially

---

### Essay 2D: What Keeps You Up at Night

**Prompt**: "What issue could you talk about for hours? How would you approach it?"

**Purpose**: Tests intellectual passion, critical thinking, depth of engagement.

**90-100**: Specific issue + nuanced thinking about complexity + approach to solving/exploring + connection to Northwestern

**Key Criteria**: (1) Deep not surface, (2) Shows genuine passion, (3) Critical thinking evident, (4) Problem-solving approach

---

### Essay 2E: Residential Experience

**Prompt**: "In terms of residential experience, what interests you most?"

**Purpose**: Tests understanding of Northwestern's residential college system and community fit.

**90-100**: Specific residential colleges/communities + understanding of what they offer + connection to goals/interests

**Key Criteria**: (1) Research specific residential options, (2) Connection to interests, (3) Community engagement mindset
